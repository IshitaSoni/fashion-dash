🔍 System Context & Architecture:

Our application, Fashion Dash, tracks user fashion archetypes and serves product recommendations. 
The codebase consists of three primary modules: 
- style_analyzer.py – Segments users into fashion dimensions based on e-commerce text descriptions.
- recommender.py – Scores items in a master catalog against the user's computed preferences.
- main.py – The orchestrator that chains these modules into a single execution pipeline.

🚨 Current Engineering Vulnerabilities (The Failures)

Our current keyword matching logic across style_analyzer.py and recommender.py relies on naive, case-insensitive substring containment checks (if keyword in title:). 
This creates two critical bugs in our system: 
- Substring Collisions (False Positives): The system triggers false matches on non-relevant items due to embedded word fragments. For example, a user who buys an item with the word "Occupants" or "Participle" accidentally triggers points for the keyword "pants" or "capri", skewing their style matrix completely.
- Compound-Modifier Over-Counting: If an item title in our catalog contains a keyword multiple times or bundles it closely with punctuation (e.g., "Oversized, baggy-style streetwear hoodie!"), the loop behavior causes multiple additive hits on a single item, pushing recommendation match scores past $100\%$ or skewing standard catalog filtration distributions.
